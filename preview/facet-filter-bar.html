Chips come only from the caller's taxonomy; domain names are a prop. Multiple active chips OR together, and the caller applies the bar's filter to the canvas. When an entity's sub-topic is shared by several of its domains and the data has no exact pairs, the chip still filters but carries a mark (≈), and the count says how many entities it may miss. The collapsible "filters ▾" toggle belongs to the explorer frame.
