Each bot turn is driven by an AnswerTurn: streamed text stays hidden until the turn is finalized with a verdict, so a withheld or refused answer never flashes its draft. Grounded answers carry the sources line (click to list them), the provenance footer and an optional explorer link; an answer with no evidence shows 0 claims · 0 episodes and neither. Withheld answers list the claims that hold up; refused answers end with the site's closing line. Notices and the disabled composer cover usage limits; the site supplies all of that copy.
