The launcher is the mascot with the antenna pulse-dot — the one sanctioned idle motion, static under reduced motion — and an ask [Q] caption that hides under 720px. The overlay is a ~460px portrait panel lifted 18 / 16 off the corner with a hairline frame and a translucent scrim; it traps focus, makes the page behind it inert and returns focus to the launcher on close. Copy here is the neutral default; a site passes its own title, hint, placeholder and disclaimer.
